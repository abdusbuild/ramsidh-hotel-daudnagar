Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

This policy explains how Ramsidh Hotel & Resorts, Daudnagar ("we", "us") handles information you share with us through this website, by phone, WhatsApp, email or at the hotel.

Information We Collect

  • Details you enter in our enquiry forms: name, phone number, email, dates, room or event type and message.
  • Guest registration details and government ID collected at check-in, as required by law.
  • Information you share when you call, email or message us.

How We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries and manage room and event bookings.
  • To complete guest registration as required by local authorities.
  • To contact you about your booking or event.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing Your Information

We share guest information only where required by law (for example, with police or government authorities for guest registration) or where needed to fulfil your booking.

WhatsApp & Third-Party Services

Our website forms do not store your data on a server — submitting a form opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation. The map on our Contact page is provided by Google Maps. Bookings made via MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda or other sites are also governed by their policies.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may keep a small session note so the booking pop-up is shown only once per visit.

Data Retention & Security

We keep registration records for as long as the law requires and take reasonable steps to protect them.

Your Rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you and request corrections, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
